## Meet Script Bot

Script Bot is the heart of Subscribr's content creation platform. Unlike traditional AI tools that require complex prompts, Script Bot engages in natural conversation to understand your vision and create scripts that sound authentically you.

<Info>
  **Revolutionary Approach**: Script Bot doesn't just generate text—it collaborates, asks questions, and refines content through dialogue.
</Info>

## Starting a Script Bot Session

### Quick Start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Script Bot">
    Click "New Script" or navigate to Script Bot from sidebar
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Channel">
    Choose which channel you're creating content for
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Mode">
    Start in Chat for ideation or Canvas for direct creation
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share Your Idea">
    Describe your video concept in natural language
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common Script Bot Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long does script generation take?">
    Average time is 12 minutes from idea to finished script, though first-time users may take 15-20 minutes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Script Bot write in different languages?">
    Yes! Script Bot supports multiple languages. Specify your language preference when starting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How does Script Bot maintain my voice?">
    It uses your channel's voice profile, analyzes previous scripts, and learns from your edits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can multiple people use Script Bot simultaneously?">
    Yes! Canvas Mode supports real-time collaboration with multiple team members.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if Script Bot doesn't understand my request?">
    Rephrase naturally or provide more context. Script Bot will ask clarifying questions if needed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Script Bot Best Practices

<Warning>
  **Remember**: Script Bot is a collaborator, not a replacement for your creativity. Your unique perspective is what makes content valuable.
</Warning>

### Do's and Don'ts

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Do">
    ✅ Provide clear context and goals
    ✅ Use natural, conversational language
    ✅ Iterate and refine outputs
    ✅ Leverage research and data
    ✅ Personalize final scripts
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Don't">
    ❌ Accept first drafts without review
    ❌ Use overly complex prompts
    ❌ Ignore your audience's needs
    ❌ Skip the personalization step
    ❌ Rely 100% on AI without your input

</Tabs>
