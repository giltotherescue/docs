Vibe Scripting represents a paradigm shift in content creation. Instead of wrestling with prompts or staring at blank pages, you simply **chat about your video idea** and watch it transform into a viral-ready script that sounds authentically you.

## How Vibe Scripting Works

### The Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Share Your Vision">
    Tell Script Bot your video idea in natural language—no special formatting needed
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collaborative Exploration">
    Script Bot asks clarifying questions to understand your goals and audience
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch It Materialize">
    Your script appears in real-time as Script Bot writes
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine Through Conversation">
    Request changes naturally: "Make it funnier" or "Add more examples"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Perfect and Export">
    Fine-tune specific sections and export in your preferred format
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Starting Your Vibe Session

### Opening Lines That Work

Instead of complex prompts, start naturally:

<CodeGroup>
  ```text Specific Idea
  "I want to make a video about why the iPhone 16 Pro
  camera is actually worse than the iPhone 15 Pro"
  ```

  ```text General Topic
  "Help me create content about productivity apps
  for students"
  ```

  ```text Series Planning
  "I'm planning a series on beginner investing -
  let's start with the first video"
  ```

  ```text Trend Response
  "There's a viral trend about morning routines -
  I want to do my take on it"
  ```
</CodeGroup>

## Script Bot's Intelligent Questions

Script Bot doesn't just generate—it collaborates. Expect questions like:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Audience Targeting">
    - "Who's your target audience for this video?"
    - "What age group are we speaking to?"
    - "What's their level of knowledge on this topic?"
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Content Goals">
    - "What's the main takeaway you want viewers to have?"
    - "Are you teaching, entertaining, or persuading?"
    - "What action should viewers take after watching?"
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Video Specifications">
    - "How long should this video be?"
    - "Is this part of a series?"
    - "What's your upload schedule?"
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Style Preferences">
    - "Casual and funny or more professional?"
    - "Fast-paced or detailed explanations?"
    - "Any specific examples or stories to include?"
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Advanced Vibe Techniques

### Layered Development

Build your script in layers for maximum control:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Structure First">
    "Let's start with just the outline and main points"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expand Sections">
    "Now develop the introduction with a strong hook"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Personality">
    "Inject more humor throughout"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Polish Details">
    "Perfect the call-to-action"
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Reference-Based Creation

Use existing content as inspiration:

<CodeGroup>
  ```text Structure Reference
  "I love how MrBeast structures his intros -
  let's do something similar for my tech review"
  ```

  ```text Tone Reference
  "Write this in a style similar to Marques Brownlee -
  technical but accessible"
  ```

  ```text Format Reference
  "Structure this like a Vox Explained video -
  building complexity gradually"
  ```
</CodeGroup>

## Common Vibe Patterns

### Successful Conversation Flows

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="The Explorer">
    ```text
    You: "I want to talk about AI but not sure what angle"
    Bot: "What aspect interests you most?"
    You: "How it's changing creative work"
    Bot: "Are you focusing on opportunities or concerns?"
    You: "Both - balanced perspective"
    Bot: "Let's structure it as a journey from fear to opportunity..."
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="The Refiner">
    ```text
    You: "Video about morning routines, 10 minutes, energetic"
    Bot: [Generates outline]
    You: "Add more science backing"
    Bot: [Adds research sections]
    You: "Make the intro hook stronger"
    Bot: [Rewrites introduction]
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="The Collaborator">
    ```text
    You: "Help me respond to that viral TikTok about..."
    Bot: "What's your take on it?"
    You: "I disagree but want to be respectful"
    Bot: "Let's acknowledge valid points first, then present your perspective..."
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Troubleshooting Vibe Sessions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Script feels generic">
    Add more specific details about your angle and unique perspective
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Wrong tone or style">
    Check your voice profile settings and provide tone examples
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Missing key points">
    Use the research sidebar to add important information
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Too long or short">
    Specify exact time target: "This needs to be exactly 8 minutes"
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
