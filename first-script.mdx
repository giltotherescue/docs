## Welcome to Your First Script!

This guide walks you through creating your very first YouTube script using Subscribr's Vibe Scripting technology. We'll create a real script together, showing you exactly what to expect at each step.

<Info>
  **Time Required**: 10-15 minutes for your first script (future scripts take ~12 minutes)
</Info>

## Before You Begin

### Quick Checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Account Ready">

## Let's Create Your Script!

### Step 1: Open Script Bot

<img
  className="rounded-lg"
  src="/images/script-bot-start.png"
  alt="Starting Script Bot"
/>

Navigate to Script Bot:
1. Click **"New Script"** button in your dashboard
2. Or select **"Script Bot"** from the left sidebar
3. Choose your channel from the dropdown

<Tip>
  First time? Script Bot will remember your preferences for next time.
</Tip>

### Step 2: Choose Your Creation Mode

You'll see two options:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Chat Mode" icon="comments">
    Start here if you want to:
    - Brainstorm ideas
    - Explore different angles
    - Get strategic advice
  </Card>
  <Card title="Canvas Mode" icon="pen-to-square">
    Jump here if you:
    - Know exactly what you want
    - Have a clear outline
    - Want to see the script immediately
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

**For your first script, let's start in Chat Mode to experience the full flow.**

### Step 3: Share Your Idea

Now for the fun part! Tell Script Bot your video idea. Here are examples of great starting messages:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Specific Topic">
    ```text
    "I want to create a 10-minute video about the
    5 best productivity apps for college students
    in 2025"
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="General Concept">
    ```text
    "Help me make content about sustainable living
    for beginners - not sure about the exact angle yet"
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Trending Response">
    ```text
    "There's a viral video about morning routines -
    I want to create my own unique take on it"
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Step 4: Answer Script Bot's Questions

Script Bot will ask 2-4 clarifying questions. Here's a real example:

<CodeGroup>
  ```text Conversation Example
  You: "I want to make a video about meal prep for busy professionals"

  Script Bot: "Great topic! Let me ask a few questions to make this perfect:

  1. How long should the video be? (5-10 minutes is typical)
  2. What's the main benefit you want viewers to get?
  3. Should this be practical tutorial or more inspirational?"

  You: "10 minutes, I want them to save 5 hours per week,
  definitely practical with real recipes"

  Script Bot: "Perfect! I'll create a practical 10-minute guide
  that shows busy professionals exactly how to meal prep
  efficiently. Let me include specific recipes and time-saving tips..."
  ```
</CodeGroup>

### Step 5: Review Your Outline

Script Bot creates an outline first. You'll see something like:

<Note>
  **Your Outline:**
  - Hook: The shocking truth about meal prep time
  - Problem: Why professionals struggle with healthy eating
  - Solution Overview: The 2-hour Sunday system
  - Section 1: Essential equipment and setup
  - Section 2: Three versatile base recipes
  - Section 3: Mix-and-match strategy
  - Section 4: Storage and reheating tips
  - Conclusion: Your action plan for this Sunday
</Note>

**Don't like something?** Just say:
- "Make the hook more dramatic"
- "Add a section about budget considerations"
- "Remove the equipment section"

### Step 6: Generate Your Full Script

Once you approve the outline, Script Bot writes your complete script. This is where the magic happens!

<img
  className="rounded-lg"
  src="/images/canvas-generation.png"
  alt="Script Generation in Canvas"
/>

You'll automatically switch to **Canvas Mode** where you see:
- **Left side**: Your ongoing chat with Script Bot
- **Right side**: Your script appearing in real-time

### Step 7: Perfect Your Script

Now comes the refinement. You can:

#### Edit Directly in Canvas
- Click any paragraph to edit manually
- Highlight text to format
- Add or remove sections

#### Ask Script Bot for Changes
Common requests that work great:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Strengthen the Hook">
    "The introduction feels weak - make it more attention-grabbing"
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Add Examples">
    "Add specific examples to the second section"
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Adjust Tone">
    "Make this more conversational and less formal"
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Include Data">
    "Add some statistics about meal prep benefits"
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Improve Flow">
    "The transition between sections 2 and 3 is choppy"
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Step 8: Final Polish

Before exporting, check these elements:

<Checklist>
  - [ ] **Hook**: Does it grab attention in the first 5 seconds?
  - [ ] **Value Clear**: Is the benefit obvious to viewers?
  - [ ] **Your Voice**: Does it sound like something you'd say?
  - [ ] **CTA**: Is there a clear call-to-action?
  - [ ] **Length**: Is it the right duration for your format?
</Checklist>

### Step 9: Export Your Script

You're done! Time to export:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Export Button">
    Find it in the top-right corner of Canvas
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Format">
    - **Word Document** - For editing offline
    - **Google Docs** - For team collaboration
    - **Teleprompter** - For recording
    - **Plain Text** - For simple needs
    - **PDF** - For sharing/printing
  </Step>
  <Step title="Additional Options">
    - Generate video description
    - Create tags and keywords
    - Export thumbnail ideas
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your Completed Script

Here's what your final script might look like:

<Expandable title="View Sample Script Output">
```text
Title: Meal Prep in 2 Hours: The Busy Professional's Guide to Eating Well

[HOOK - 0:00-0:15]
What if I told you that spending just 2 hours this Sunday could save you
5 hours next week AND help you eat healthier than ever? I'm about to show
you the exact meal prep system that's helped me and thousands of other
busy professionals transform their eating habits without sacrificing their
schedule. Let's dive in.

[INTRODUCTION - 0:15-0:45]
If you're watching this, you're probably tired of spending $15 on sad desk
salads or hitting the drive-through because you didn't have time to cook.
I get it. As someone who works 60-hour weeks, I've been there. But two
years ago, I discovered a meal prep system that changed everything...

[MAIN CONTENT - 0:45-8:30]
[Continues with full script...]

[CONCLUSION - 8:30-10:00]
So there you have it - your complete 2-hour meal prep system. This Sunday,
instead of dreading Monday's lunch scramble, spend 2 hours in your kitchen
and set yourself up for a week of healthy, delicious meals...
```
</Expandable>

## Common First Script Mistakes

<Warning>
  Avoid these pitfalls that new users often encounter:
</Warning>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Being Too Vague" icon="cloud">
    **Problem**: "Make a video about fitness"
    **Solution**: "Create a 10-minute beginner's guide to starting strength training at home"
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Ignoring Your Audience" icon="users-slash">
    **Problem**: Not specifying who you're talking to
    **Solution**: Always define your target viewer clearly
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Accepting First Draft" icon="check">
    **Problem**: Not refining the initial output
    **Solution**: Always make 2-3 refinement requests
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Wrong Voice Settings" icon="microphone-slash">
    **Problem**: Script sounds generic
    **Solution**: Configure your voice profile before starting
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
