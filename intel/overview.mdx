## Key Metrics Explained

### Views/Subscriber (V/S) Ratio

<Note>
  The V/S ratio reveals content that appeals beyond a channel's existing audience—perfect for finding viral formulas.
</Note>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="What It Means">
    - **< 0.5**: Underperforming
    - **0.5-1.0**: Meeting expectations
    - **1.0-3.0**: Strong performance
    - **3.0+**: Viral/Breakout content
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="How to Use It">
    - Find high V/S videos in your niche
    - Analyze what made them break out
    - Apply similar angles to your content
    - Target broader audiences
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Outlier Score

Videos that dramatically exceed channel averages:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Calculation">
    Compared to channel's 30-day average:
    - Views
    - Engagement
    - Growth rate
    - Comments
  </Step>
  <Step title="Interpretation">
    - **2x-3x**: Strong performer
    - **3x-5x**: Significant outlier
    - **5x+**: Viral phenomenon
  </Step>
  <Step title="Application">
    Study outliers to understand:
    - Winning topics
    - Effective formats
    - Perfect timing
    - Audience triggers
  </Step>
</Steps>
