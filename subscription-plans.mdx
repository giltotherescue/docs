### Credit Management

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Credit Validity" icon="calendar">
    - **Monthly plans**: Credits expire after 60 days (current + next month)
    - **Annual plans**: Credits valid for entire subscription period
    - **Unused credits**: Don't fully roll over (use them!)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Running Low?" icon="battery-low">
    - Purchase add-on credit packs
    - Upgrade your plan mid-cycle
    - Credits from upgrades are prorated
    - Contact support for volume discounts
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Team Credits" icon="users">
    - Credits are shared across your team
    - Track usage by member in dashboard
    - Set credit limits per user (Scale plans)
    - Get alerts when running low
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Choosing the Right Plan

### Plan Selection Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Estimate Your Volume">
    How many scripts do you plan to create monthly?
    - 1-5 scripts: Ideation
    - 5-15 scripts: Creator
    - 15-30 scripts: Automation
    - 30+ scripts: Scale
  </Step>
  <Step title="Consider Your Needs">
    What features are essential?
    - Just scripting: Ideation
    - Need Intel: Creator+
    - Team collaboration: Automation+
    - Enterprise needs: Scale
  </Step>
  <Step title="Factor Growth">
    Plan for your channel's growth:
    - Starting out: Begin with Ideation
    - Growing fast: Start with Creator
    - Established: Automation or Scale
  </Step>
</Steps>
