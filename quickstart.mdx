## Welcome to Subscribr

This guide will walk you through creating your first YouTube script using Subscribr's revolutionary **Vibe Scripting** technology. In just a few minutes, you'll have a professional script ready for production.

## Step 1: Create Your First Channel

Channels are workspaces in Subscribr where you organize your content, define your voice, and manage your scripts.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Channels">
    Click on **Channels** in the left sidebar of your dashboard
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Channel">
    Select **"Create New Channel"** button
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Channel Type">
    - **Link YouTube Channel**: Connect an existing YouTube channel to import data and personalize AI
    - **Create Custom Channel**: Start fresh without linking to YouTube (perfect for planning)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Basic Settings">
    - Enter your channel name
    - Select your primary content category
    - Choose your target audience demographic
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Linking your YouTube channel allows Subscribr to analyze your existing content and create a personalized AI that matches your style perfectly.
</Tip>

## Step 2: Start Script Bot

Script Bot is your AI collaborator that transforms conversations into viral-ready scripts.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Script Bot">
    Click the **"New Script"** button or navigate to **Script Bot** from the sidebar
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Channel">
    Choose the channel you just created from the dropdown
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Mode">
    - **Chat Mode**: For brainstorming and ideation
    - **Canvas Mode**: For real-time script editing (recommended for quick starts)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 3: Create Your First Script

Let's create a script using the revolutionary Vibe Scripting approach.

### Method 1: Natural Conversation (Recommended)

<CodeGroup>
  ```text Example Conversation
  You: "I want to make a video about the top 5 productivity apps for students"

  Script Bot: "Great topic! Let me ask a few questions to make this perfect:
  1. What's your target video length? (5-10 minutes?)
  2. Are you targeting high school or college students?
  3. Do you want a casual or more professional tone?"

  You: "10 minutes, college students, casual but informative"

  Script Bot: "Perfect! I'll create an engaging script that speaks directly to college students..."
  ```
</CodeGroup>

### Method 2: Quick Template

If you prefer structure, use a template:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Educational">
    ```text
    "Create a 10-minute educational video about [TOPIC]
    Target audience: [DEMOGRAPHIC]
    Tone: Informative but engaging
    Include: Introduction, 3-5 main points, conclusion with CTA"
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Review">
    ```text
    "Create a product review for [PRODUCT]
    Length: 8-10 minutes
    Include: Unboxing, features, pros/cons, verdict
    Tone: Honest and conversational"
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Tutorial">
    ```text
    "Create a how-to tutorial for [SKILL/TASK]
    Include: What viewers will learn, step-by-step process, common mistakes
    Length: 10-15 minutes"
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Step 4: Watch Your Script Come to Life

Once you provide your initial idea, Script Bot will:

1. **Generate an outline** based on proven YouTube formulas
2. **Write your complete script** section by section
3. **Apply your channel voice** to maintain consistency
4. **Optimize for engagement** with hooks and retention tactics

<Note>
  The script appears in real-time in Canvas mode. You can edit any section by clicking on it or asking Script Bot to revise specific parts.
</Note>

## Step 5: Refine and Perfect

### Real-Time Editing
- Click any paragraph to edit directly
- Ask Script Bot to revise specific sections
- Request tone adjustments or length changes

### Common Refinement Commands

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Make it more engaging">

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Adjust the tone">

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Add examples">

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Step 6: Export Your Script

Once you're satisfied with your script:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Export">
    Find the **Export** button in the top right corner
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Format">
    - **Word Document** (.docx) - For editing in Microsoft Word
    - **Google Docs** - Direct export to your Google Drive
    - **Plain Text** (.txt) - For simple text editors
    - **Teleprompter** - Formatted for teleprompter apps
    - **PDF** - For sharing and printing
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download or Share">
    Download to your device or share directly with team members
  </Step>
</Steps>
