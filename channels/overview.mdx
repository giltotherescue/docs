## Understanding Channels

Channels are the foundation of your Subscribr workspace. Each channel represents a unique content identity with its own voice, audience, and strategy. Whether you're managing one YouTube channel or dozens, Subscribr's channel system keeps everything organized and optimized.

<Info>
  **Key Concept**: One Subscribr channel = One content identity (which may or may not be linked to an actual YouTube channel)
</Info>

## Types of Channels

### 1. Linked YouTube Channel

<Card title="Connect Your Existing YouTube Channel" icon="youtube" color="#FF0000">
  **Best for:** Established creators wanting personalized AI

  When you link your YouTube channel, Subscribr:
  - Imports your video library and analyzes content patterns
  - Studies your writing style from existing scripts
  - Understands your audience demographics
  - Tracks performance metrics
  - Personalizes AI to match your established voice
  - Provides tailored recommendations
</Card>

### 2. Custom Channel

<Card title="Create a Planning Workspace" icon="plus" color="#0D9488">
  **Best for:** New channels, testing ideas, or non-YouTube content

  Custom channels let you:
  - Plan content before launching on YouTube
  - Test different content strategies
  - Create content for other platforms
  - Develop unique voice profiles
  - Build audience personas from scratch
  - Experiment without affecting existing channels
</Card>

## Troubleshooting Channels

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can't link YouTube channel">
    Ensure you're logged into the correct Google account and have granted necessary permissions
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Voice profile not working">
    Check that you've selected the correct voice profile in channel settings
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Team can't access channel">
    Verify team member permissions and channel visibility settings
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Analytics not updating">
    Linked channel data updates every 24 hours; check connection status
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
